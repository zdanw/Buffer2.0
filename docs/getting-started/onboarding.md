# Onboarding wizard & checklist

PulseForge guides new users with a modal wizard and a persistent checklist.

![Login / first visit](../frontend/public/docs/screenshots/login.png)

## Onboarding wizard

Shown after your **first login** until you skip or finish.

| Step | What you do |
|------|-------------|
| Welcome | Overview — click **Get started** |
| Quick brand | Brand name + voice, or **No specific brand (Generic)** |
| Quick product | Product name + upload at least one image |
| Done | **Go to Studio** to generate your first post |

**Skip for now** dismisses the wizard. Your account works normally; open **Help → Quick start** anytime.

## Getting started checklist

A floating panel in the **bottom-right** tracks:

1. ✅ Create your account (auto-complete)
2. Create a brand kit
3. Add a product
4. Generate your first post

Click incomplete steps to navigate directly. The checklist hides when all four are done.

### Optional links in the checklist

- **Help Center** — full user guide
- **Automations** — schedule recurring generation

## Onboarding reward

Complete **brand + product + first generation** to receive bonus **free image credits** (shown as a toast notification).

## Generic vs named brand

- **Generic** — neutral voice, no Buffer binding required to experiment
- **Named brand kit** — consistent tone, hashtags, logo rules, and Buffer account binding

Create a real brand kit before scaling to many products or auto-publish.
