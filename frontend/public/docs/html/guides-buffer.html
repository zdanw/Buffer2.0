Buffer setup

Postence publishes through Buffer. You need a Buffer account with connected social profiles.

Steps

In Buffer: **Account → Developers**

In Postence: **Buffer accounts**

**Brand kits**

Publish from Studio, Review, or enable auto publish on Automations

In-app: open Buffer accounts and follow the built-in token guide.
